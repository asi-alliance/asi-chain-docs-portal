# Get DevNet ASI

DevNet ASI is a test token for trying out transfers and contract deployments on the ASI:Chain DevNet.

::: warning DevNet tokens have no value
They exist for testing only and cannot be exchanged for real currency.
:::

## Steps

1. Get an address. Open [wallet.dev.asichain.io](https://wallet.dev.asichain.io), create a wallet or import one, and copy the ASI address of your account. The address starts with `1111`. [Wallet Usage](/wallet/usage/) describes each screen.
2. Request tokens. Open [faucet.dev.asichain.io](https://faucet.dev.asichain.io), paste the address into the **Address** field and press **FAUCET**. The faucet sends 10,000 ASI to an address whose balance is not above 20,000 ASI.
3. Wait for `Finalized`. The **TX status check** section shows the status of the transfer and refreshes it every 30 seconds. [Faucet Usage](/faucet/usage/) lists all statuses and error messages.
4. Check the balance. The faucet shows the new balance under **Address Balance**, and the wallet shows it on its Wallet page.

## Next

With a balance, you can send tokens on the wallet's **Send** page, deploy a contract on its **Deploy** page, or look up your transactions in [explorer.dev.asichain.io](https://explorer.dev.asichain.io).
